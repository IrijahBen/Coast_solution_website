# Getting Started with Coast BMS

Welcome to Coast BMS. This guide covers everything you need to initialize your first store register and manage inventory ledgers.

## 1. Initializing Your Register
Navigate to your active dashboard and select **Launch POS Terminal**. Ensure your cashier profile has appropriate permissions configured.

## 2. Inventory Synchronization
All SKUs added to your central ledger automatically propagate across your connected branch locations in real time.
